# Summer (Year 1)

## When this matters
- From Spring 28 (plan the Summer 1 purchase) until Fall 1. Summer is the first season where one crop (Blueberry) carries the whole income and where machines and sprinklers start to replace hand labor.

## Priorities
1. **Blueberries on every tile you can water, planted Summer 1-3.**
   - Pierre is the only regular seller of Blueberry seeds (80g); Joja has none. Summer 3 is a Wednesday and Pierre is closed, so buy the full batch on Summer 1 or 2.
   - Summer 1-3 planting gives 4 harvests of 3 berries; Summer 4-7 gives 3; after Summer 15 none. Each plant regrows every 4 days, so the plot is a fixed watering cost all season: size it to your watering energy, not your cash.
   - Speed-Gro at planting cuts 2 days: on Summer 1 it gives a 5th harvest, and it keeps 4 harvests possible up to Summer 5. Buy it if cash allows after the seeds.
2. **Bundle crops in small amounts.** Plant a few Tomato and Hot Pepper, a row of Corn and a small Melon batch on Summer 1-2.
   - The Summer Crops bundle (Tomato, Hot Pepper, Blueberry, Melon) pays a Quality Sprinkler.
   - Quality Crops needs 5 gold each of 3 of Parsnip, Melon, Pumpkin, Corn. Corn regrows every 4 days and survives into Fall, so it gives the most gold rolls; Melon is the second pick.
   - Put Basic Fertilizer on Corn and Melon tiles: at Farming 4-6 it roughly doubles the gold chance.
3. **Melon timing.** Melons take 12 days: Summer 1 → 13 → 25. Last planting Summer 16. With Speed-Gro a Summer 1 Melon is ripe the morning of Summer 11 (the Luau); plant 2-3 that way only if you have no other gold item worth 160g+ for the soup (Speed-Gro tiles cannot also take Basic Fertilizer, so gold is a gamble). Luau rules: see `guide('festivals')`.
4. **Preserves Jars at Farming 4.** Blueberry Jelly sells for 3x the raw berry. Build Jars as fast as Wood, Stone and Coal allow and keep them full of normal-quality berries; ship silver and gold berries raw (machines ignore quality). You will never have enough Jars for the whole crop, so do not hold berries back waiting for one.
5. **Mine for Iron and Gold, then Quality Sprinklers at Farming 6.** Summer 13 and 26 are always thunderstorms and the Green Rain day waters too; any other day can be dry, so watering is still the daily bottleneck. Each Quality Sprinkler waters the 8 tiles around it and removes that energy cost for good (recipe: see `guide('money')`). Mine on days the crops do not need you: Railroad Spa opens Summer 3 and refills energy for free. Depth targets: see `guide('mines')`.
6. **Fishing goals.**
   - Pufferfish (Specialty Fish bundle): Beach, sunny, 12:00-16:00, summer only in the Valley (Ginger Island later). Catch it this summer.
   - Sunfish (River Fish bundle): river, sunny, gone after Summer.
   - Rain days (Summer 13 and 26, the Green Rain day, random rain) are for bundle fish: Shad in the river, Red Snapper in the ocean.
   - Crimsonfish (legendary, once only): Fishing 5, far east end of the Beach pier, summer only.
   - Trout Derby on Summer 20-21: see `guide('festivals')`. Rods, bait and crab pots: see `guide('fishing')`.
7. **Use the Green Rain day.** One summer day (5, 6, 7, 14, 15, 16, 18 or 23; the TV forecast shows it the day before) is a special rain: crops are watered, young trees jump to full size and about half the trees grow Moss. Collect Moss for Speed-Gro (Farming 3: Pine Tar + 5 Moss makes 5; Pine Tar needs a Tapper on a Pine). Clear the extra weeds and bushes that day for Fiber: they vanish tomorrow. In Year 1 Robin pauses construction that day, so a build ends one day later.
8. **Storm days.** Summer 13 and 26 are always thunderstorms: skip watering, fish Shad and Red Snapper, and expect lightning to kill a few crops unless Lightning Rods stand nearby.
9. **Last week.** Fall 1 kills every summer-only crop; Corn, Sunflower and Wheat survive. After Summer 16 plant only crops that finish by Summer 28 (`crops` shows which). Harvest everything on Summer 28.
10. **Cash reserve for Fall 1.** From about Summer 20 stop optional spending. On Fall 1 you need Cranberry seeds (240g each) for every tile you can water, plus some Pumpkins; see `guide('fall')`. If cash is still spare, buy a Rare Seed at the Traveling Cart for Fall 1 (see `guide('money')`).
11. **Farm cave.** Once your total earnings pass 25,000g, Demetrius visits the farm one morning and offers to fill the cave: choose Mushrooms. Red, Purple and Morel mushrooms fill Exotic Foraging, Dye and Field Research slots and feed Life Elixirs; bats drop mostly cheap berries.

## Keep / Don't sell
- One each: Tomato, Hot Pepper, Blueberry, Melon (Summer Crops).
- 5 gold Corn and 5 gold Melon (Quality Crops), unless the bundle is already done with Parsnips or later Pumpkins. Keep one normal Corn for the Fall Crops bundle.
- One each forage: Spice Berry, Grape, Sweet Pea (Summer Foraging). Spice Berry and Sweet Pea are summer only; Grape also grows in Fall.
- One gold edible item (not a Pufferfish) with base price 160g+ for the Luau (Summer 11) until the Luau is over.
- Bulletin Board: 1 Poppy, 1 Sunflower, 10 Wheat, 1 Red Mushroom, 1 Sea Urchin.
- One of each bundle fish caught (check `bundles(action='keep')`); these bite now (Pufferfish and Sunfish last in Summer): Pufferfish, Sunfish, Shad, Red Snapper, Sturgeon, Tuna, Tilapia.
- Wood, Stone, Coal, Copper/Iron/Gold ore and Refined Quartz: Jars and sprinklers eat all of it.

## Avoid
- Hops without Kegs. Raw Hops sell for 25g and only pay in a Keg (Farming 8) or a Jar, and your Jars belong to Blueberries. The trellis also blocks walking: leave a path if you plant any.
- Starfruit in Year 1. Seeds are sold only at the Oasis (needs the bus, Vault bundles). Do not buy the single 3,000g Starfruit at Pierre's Luau stall: at normal quality it does not reach the best soup tier.
- Planting Blueberries after Summer 15, Melons after Summer 16, or anything that cannot ripen by Summer 28.
- Shopping on Wednesdays (Summer 3, 10, 17, 24): Pierre is closed until the Community Center is restored. Summer 10 and the Luau day are both closed, so restock seeds and fertilizer by Summer 9.
- Kegging Blueberries: a Jar gives about the same price in under half the time.
- Spending the Fall 1 seed money on decor, furniture or a backpack in late Summer.

## Look it up
- `crops(season='summer')` and `crops(season='summer', fertilizer='speed_gro')`: harvests left from today, seed prices at every shop, profit per tile.
- `bundles(room='Pantry', missing_only=true)` and `bundles(action='keep')`: what still needs to be kept before you ship.
- `machines(item='Blueberry')`: Jar vs Keg value; `machines()`: which placed Jars are ready.
- `fish_info()` on the spot, `fish_info(season='summer', weather='rain')`, `fish_info(fish='Crimsonfish')`.
- `progress()`: Farming XP to levels 4 and 6, bars in pack and chests, mine depth.
- `calendar()`: festival days and birthdays; `item_info('Rare Seed')`: cart price and growth.
