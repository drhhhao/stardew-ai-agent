# Fishing: rods, bait, where to fish, ponds

## When this matters
- Spring 2-4 (income without seed money), every rainy day, and any day the farm needs no work.
- Before buying a rod, tackle, crab pots, a Fish Pond or the Fish Smoker, and before selling any fish.

## Priorities
1. **Opening.**
   - Spring 2: Willy's letter arrives. Visit the Beach before 17:00 for the free Bamboo Pole. Skip the Training Rod: it only hooks easy fish and every catch is normal quality.
   - At Fishing 2 Willy sells the Fiberglass Rod (1,800g). Buy it that day: it holds bait, and bait makes fish bite much faster.
   - **Always fish with bait.** Bait costs 5g, or craft 5 from 1 Bug Meat (Fishing 2). Buy 100+ at a time so a closed shop never stops you.
   - Never buy Deluxe Bait (100g each). Craft it from 5 Bait + 2 Moss (Fishing 4), or take the 30 from the River Fish bundle.
2. **Rainy days are fishing days.** Rain waters the crops, and five bundle fish bite only in rain (snow does not count).
   - Run `fish_info(weather='rain')` and catch every missing rain fish before anything else (the list is in `guide('community_center')`).
   - Rainy-day route: rivers in the morning (Catfish), then the Beach in the evening (Eel).
   - Once reachable (Secret Woods: Steel Axe; Witch's Swamp: after the Goblin Problem quest), both have Catfish on any rainy day, Summer included.
3. **Fishing 3: craft crab pots and learn Dish O' The Sea.** See Crab pots below. Dish O' The Sea (2 Sardine + 1 Hashbrowns) gives +3 Fishing.
4. **Fishing 6: buy the Iridium Rod (7,500g)** once the season's seed money is safe. It holds bait plus one tackle. Tackle lasts about 20 catches.
   - **Trap Bobber** (fish escapes more slowly) or **Cork Bobber** (Fishing 7, bigger bar): use one while `fish` still loses fish, and always for legendaries.
   - Once `fish` lands nearly everything, switch to **Spinner** (faster bites) or **Treasure Hunter** (more chests).
   - Craft Trap and Cork Bobbers. Buy Spinners and Treasure Hunters instead of spending Iron or Gold Bars that sprinklers need.
   - Shop unlocks use your base Fishing level; food buffs do not count.
5. **Top picks per season.** Run `fish_info()` on the spot for the exact list.
   - Spring: sunny, Largemouth Bass (Mountain lake) or Flounder (Beach); rain, Catfish (Town or Forest river); rainy evening, Eel (Beach).
   - Summer: Pufferfish (Beach, sunny, 12:00-16:00), Octopus (Beach, before 13:00), Sturgeon (Mountain lake), Super Cucumber (Beach, after 18:00).
   - Fall: Tiger Trout (rivers), Catfish and Walleye in rain, Super Cucumber (Beach, after 18:00), Midnight Carp (Mountain lake or Forest pond, after 22:00).
   - Winter: Sturgeon (Mountain lake), Tiger Trout and Lingcod (rivers), Squid (Beach, after 18:00), Midnight Carp (after 22:00).
   - Winter never rains on its own. A Rain Totem forces rain; then Catfish and Walleye (from 12:00) bite in the rivers. Use one if a rain bundle fish is still missing.
   - When a fish frenzy is announced for a valuable or missing fish, go there: `fish` targets the bubbles.
6. **Mine fish.** The lake floors are 20, 60 and 100: Stonefish on 20, Ice Pip on 60, Lava Eel on 100; Ghostfish on 20 and 60.
   - The chance rises with Fishing level and with deep casts. Matching targeted bait raises it a lot.
   - On floor 100, almost every cast that is not a Lava Eel is trash. Fish there only with Lava Eel bait.
   - Willy stocks one random targeted bait a day (8-12 of it) from a seasonal list: Stonefish bait can appear in Spring and Winter, Ice Pip in Summer and Winter, Lava Eel in Fall and Winter. Buy it when it shows up. The Bait Maker (Fishing 6: 3 Iron Bars, 3 Coral, 1 Sea Urchin) makes it from any fish you already have.
   - Fish there only when already on that floor with spare energy.
7. **Legendaries.** One catch each per save; Qi's Extended Family order later lets you catch their relatives at the same spots. Bring the Iridium Rod with a Trap or Cork Bobber.

   | Fish | Where | When | Fishing |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Angler | Town river from its north end (the plank bridge north of JojaMart) | Fall | 3 |
   | Crimsonfish | far east Beach pier (after the 300-Wood bridge repair), 3+ tiles out | Summer | 5 |
   | Glacierfish | south tip of Arrowhead Island, Cindersap Forest; one exact tile, 3+ tiles out | Winter | 6 |
   | Legend | Mountain lake, 4+ tiles from every shore | Spring, rain only | 10 |
   | Mutant Carp | Sewers (Rusty Key: 60 museum donations) | any | none |

   - The level check counts food buffs. Dish O' The Sea (+3) gets Crimsonfish and Glacierfish at Fishing 3 and the Legend at Fishing 7. Food buffs replace each other; only one counts.
   - Below the level or off the exact spot, they never bite. Out of season they bite only with Magic Bait.
   - **Pond them instead of selling** (see priority 9).
8. **Crab pots: craft them; they are for the bundle.** Recipe at Fishing 3: 40 Wood + 3 Iron Bars. Never buy them (1,500g).
   - The Crab Pot bundle needs 5 of 10. Clam, Cockle, Mussel and Oyster are beach forage, so one pot in the ocean finishes it.
   - Placing, baiting and emptying cost no energy, but each pot needs new bait every day.
   - Ocean pots (Beach) catch Lobster, Crab and Shrimp. Freshwater pots (farm water, rivers) catch Crayfish, Snail and Periwinkle.
   - Deluxe Bait halves trash and makes silver catches. Wild Bait halves trash and sometimes doubles the catch.
   - Extra pots give passive Fishing XP and little money.
9. **Fish Ponds** (Robin: 5,000g, 200 Stone, 5 Seaweed, 5 Green Algae; 2 days). One species per pond. A pond grows toward 10 fish when you deliver the item it asks for.
   - **Sturgeon:** its Roe in a Preserves Jar becomes Caviar (500g). The best normal pond.
   - **Lava Eel:** high-value Roe; Aged Roe from a Jar is worth double.
   - **Legendary:** capacity 1, Roe on about half the days. Roe is worth 30g plus half the fish's price, so a Legend pond beats the one-time sale within days.
   - Pond the cheapest copy of a fish; the pond ignores quality. Sell the gold and iridium ones.
10. **Fish Smoker.** Recipe 10,000g from Willy; craft it from 10 Hardwood + Sea Jelly + River Jelly + Cave Jelly.
    - It doubles a fish's price, keeps its quality and takes 1 Coal and under an hour per fish.
    - Fisher/Angler and Artisan bonuses both apply to smoked fish.
    - Buy it once you fish daily and have a Coal supply. Smoke fish worth 100g or more; keep legendaries for ponds.
11. **Treasure chests.** `fish` collects them; what does not fit drops at your feet, so pick it up before leaving. `fish` stops when the inventory is full: start every trip with free slots.
12. **Energy and time.** Each cast costs 8 energy (a little less at higher Fishing levels), so a full bar is only a few dozen casts: carry food. `fish` stops after 00:30; be in bed before 2:00 or you pass out.

## Keep / Don't sell
- One of each fish an open bundle needs, at any quality unless `bundles` asks for gold. Rain-only fish first: missing one costs a season or a year.
- Sea Jelly, River Jelly and Cave Jelly until the Fish Smoker is built.
- 5 Seaweed and 5 Green Algae per planned Fish Pond.
- Sardines for Dish O' The Sea; Bug Meat for bait; Coral and Sea Urchin for the Bait Maker.
- Every legendary, for its pond.
- Trash only if you own a Recycling Machine (Fishing 4); otherwise discard it.

## Avoid
- Fishing without bait after Fishing 2.
- Buying Crab Pots or Deluxe Bait.
- Selling the last copy of a bundle fish.
- Cooking bundle fish or 100g+ fish into energy food; use Carp, Sardine, Herring, Bream or Anchovy.
- Fishing floor 100 without Lava Eel bait.
- Staying out past 2:00, or casting far from home with no food.

## Look it up
- `fish_info()`: what bites here now, with hours, weather, price and bundle need.
- `fish_info(fish='Catfish')`, `fish_info(season='fall', weather='rain')`, `fish_info(location='mines')`: where and when a fish bites, rain fish, mine fish, crab-pot catches.
- `item_info(name)`: sell price with your professions, smoked value, recipes.
- `machines(item='Sturgeon Roe')`, `machines(machine='Fish Smoker')`: outputs and the state of your machines.
- `bundles(room='Fish Tank', missing_only=true)` and `bundles(action=keep)`: which fish to keep.
- `progress()`: Fishing level, XP to the next level and recipe unlocks.
