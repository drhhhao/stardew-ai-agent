# Fall: the big harvest and getting ready for Winter

## When this matters
- Fall 1-28, mostly Year 1; the crop plan works in every later fall too.
- Fall is the biggest crop payday of Year 1 and the last outdoor season: Winter 1 kills every outdoor crop, and Winter has no natural rain.

## Priorities
1. **Fall 1: plant Cranberries on most of your waterable tiles.** They mature in 7 days and then give 2 berries every 5 days.
   - A Fall 1 planting harvests on Fall 8, 13, 18, 23 and 28 (5 harvests). Fall 2-6 gets only 4.
   - Speed-Gro adds a harvest only to Fall 2, 7, 12 or 17 plantings; otherwise use Basic Fertilizer. Deluxe Speed-Gro gives 5 harvests to plantings up to Fall 3.
   - Never plant Cranberries after Fall 16 (Fall 17 only with Speed-Gro): later ones do not repay the seed.
2. **Fall 1: Pumpkins on the next block of tiles.** 13 days, one harvest, high price, can grow giant.
   - Plant on Fall 1, harvest and replant on Fall 14, harvest on Fall 27. The last planting day is Fall 15.
   - Deluxe Speed-Gro (Sandy, Thursdays, once the bus runs; or crafted at Farming 8) makes it 9 days: Fall 1 Pumpkins then give 3 harvests (Fall 10, 19, 28). Plain Speed-Gro still gives only 2.
   - Plant in 3x3 blocks: a giant Pumpkin drops 15-21 Pumpkins.
3. **Small plots for the bundles** (Fall Crops: Corn, Eggplant, Pumpkin, Yam; check `bundles`, remixed bundles differ).
   - Corn: keep the Summer Corn alive; it carries into Fall and keeps regrowing. Fresh Corn seed bought in Fall barely repays itself, so plant a few only if you own none (by Fall 14).
   - Eggplant (5 days, regrows every 5) and Yam (10 days, plant by Fall 18): a few tiles each on Fall 1.
   - Grape (trellis, 10 days, then every 3) also fills the Summer Foraging Grape slot if it is still empty. Leave a walking path; vines block movement.
   - Sunflower for the Dye bundle, 10 Wheat for the Fodder bundle (4 days).
4. **Grow gold Pumpkins and Corn for Quality Crops** if it is open: it takes 3 of Parsnip, Melon, Pumpkin and Corn (5 gold each); grow the slots still missing. Craft Basic Fertilizer (2 Sap each, Farming 1) and put it under those tiles before planting; gold odds rise with Farming level.
5. **Fall 1-4: plant any Rare Seed** you bought from the Traveling Cart in Summer (Sweet Gem Berry, 24 days). The cart does not sell it in Fall; see `guide('money')`.
6. **Fall 8-11: shake every Blackberry bush you pass.** Also pick up Common Mushroom, Wild Plum and Hazelnut. All four fill the Fall Foraging bundle; spare Blackberries are cheap energy food.
7. **Every rainy Fall day: fish for missing rain fish.** Fall rain is the last natural rain of Year 1 (only a Rain Totem brings rain in Winter). Walleye (from noon), Catfish, Shad, Eel (ocean, evening) and Red Snapper need rain; get one of each that a bundle still lacks. The Angler legendary bites only in Fall; see `guide('fishing')`. With nothing missing, go to the Mines instead.
8. **Fall 15: shop and prepare the Fair.** Pierre is shut on Fall 16 (Fair) and Fall 17 (Wednesday, until the Community Center is restored), so buy late seeds and fertilizer by Fall 15, or pay JojaMart's 25% markup on Fall 17. Put your grange items in one chest. Fair rules and the Stardrop plan: see `guide('festivals')`.
9. **After Fall 16, plant only fast crops that finish by Fall 28:** Bok Choy or Wheat (4 days, by Fall 24), Eggplant (by Fall 23), Amaranth (7 days, by Fall 21). Ask `crops('fall')` for the exact last days.
10. **Fall 27: Spirit's Eve (evening).** Harvest the second Pumpkin crop first. In odd years (Year 1, 3...) the maze chest holds a Golden Pumpkin; route in `guide('festivals')`.
11. **Fall 28: final harvest.** Then hand the Watering Can to Clint; nothing outdoors needs water any more (skip this if the Greenhouse is open). If the Greenhouse is open or the Pantry is nearly done, buy its Fall seeds now; Pierre sells only the current season's seeds.
12. **Before Winter, finish these:**
   - **Silo before any animal.** Animals stay inside all Winter and eat 1 Hay each per day: 28 Hay per animal. One Silo holds 240 Hay, enough for 8 animals.
   - **Fill the Silo by cutting grass with the Scythe during Fall.** Grass gives Hay only once a Silo stands, does not regrow in Winter, and gives a third as much Hay there. Bought Hay costs 50g each at Marnie; buy only the shortfall.
   - **Tappers on Oak trees now** (Foraging 4). Each Keg needs 1 Oak Resin, an Oak gives 1 every 7 days, and Tappers keep producing through Winter. Kegs unlock at Farming 8; build Jars meanwhile for Cranberries and Fall vegetables (see `guide('money')`).
   - **Mines floor 120 is the Fall target.** If you will miss it, stock food and bars for a Winter push; see `guide('mines')` and `guide('winter')`.
   - **Winter forage** for the Crafts Room appears only in Winter; see `guide('winter')`.
   - **End Fall with cash** for the Winter tool upgrades and the Night Market (see `guide('winter')`); Winter has no crop income.

## Keep / Don't sell
- One each of Corn, Eggplant, Pumpkin and Yam until Fall Crops is done.
- 5 gold Pumpkins and 5 gold Corn while those Quality Crops slots are still needed.
- Grape (Summer Foraging), Sunflower (Dye), 10 Wheat and 10 Hay (Fodder) if those bundles are open.
- One each of Common Mushroom, Wild Plum, Hazelnut and Blackberry.
- Every rain fish a bundle lacks. Walleye is the costliest miss: its next chance is next Fall (or Winter with a Rain Totem). Shad and Eel return in Spring rain; Catfish also bites in Secret Woods rain in any season.
- Your best item in each grange category until the Fair on Fall 16.
- Oak Resin, Maple Syrup and Pine Tar, plus Stone, Clay and Copper Bars for the Silo.

## Avoid
- Planting Cranberries after Fall 16 (17 with Speed-Gro), Pumpkins after Fall 15, or anything that cannot be harvested by Fall 28.
- Selling the last bundle crop or the Fair items before Fall 16.
- Buying animals without a Silo, or buying a whole Winter of Hay when Fall grass is free.
- Clearing grass with the Scythe before the Silo is built: that Hay is lost.
- Upgrading the Watering Can before Fall 28 while crops still need water.
- A big Corn field for money. Grape roughly ties Pumpkin per tile but blocks paths; Cranberries pay most per tile.

## Look it up
- `crops('fall')` (add `fertilizer` to compare): seed prices in every shop, days, harvest count and last planting day for each Fall crop.
- `bundles(missing_only=true)` and `bundles(action=keep)`: what to keep before you ship.
- `fish_info(season='fall', weather='rain')`: which rain fish bite where and when.
- `item_info('Hay')`, `item_info('Golden Pumpkin')`: prices, sources and bundle uses; `villagers(item='Golden Pumpkin')` for who loves it.
- `machines(item='Cranberries')`: Jar vs Keg value for your harvest.
- `progress()`: Farming level and XP to Keg (Farming 8), Foraging level for Tappers.
- `calendar`: festival days and birthdays; `list_animals()`: Hay in the Silo and animal count.
