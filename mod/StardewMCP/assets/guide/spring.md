# Spring, Year 1

## When this matters
- Read on Spring 1 of Year 1 and again before Day 13. The first two weeks serve one goal: finish the Spring Crops Bundle by the night of Day 13 and plant Egg Festival Strawberries on its 20 Speed-Gro.
- Spring 1 is a Monday. Pierre is closed on Wednesdays (Days 3, 10, 17, 24). In Year 1, Days 1, 2 and 4 are always dry and Day 3 always rains.

## Priorities
1. **Work back from Day 13.** The Spring Crops Bundle takes 1 Parsnip, 1 Green Bean, 1 Cauliflower and 1 Potato and pays 20 Speed-Gro.
   - Cauliflower grows 12 days: buy and plant it on Day 1 (ready Day 13). A Day-2 Cauliflower misses the festival night.
   - Green Bean grows 10 days: plant it by Day 3, so buy it on Day 1 or 2 (Pierre is shut on Day 3).
   - Potato grows 6 days: plant it by Day 7.
   - Parsnip comes from the free starter seeds.
2. **Days 1-5.**
   - Day 1: you start with 500g and 15 Parsnip Seeds. Buy 1 Cauliflower Seeds (80g) and 1 Bean Starter (60g). Plant 13 Parsnips plus those two: 15 crops in total. Crows only raid farms with 16 or more crops, and nothing protects you before the Scarecrow. Keep the 2 spare Parsnip Seeds. Gather 50 Wood and craft a Chest next to the house.
   - Day 2: Willy's letter arrives. Get the free Bamboo Pole at the Beach Fish Shop and fish for the rest of the day (see `guide('fishing')`). Hoe artifact spots you pass.
   - Day 3 (rain, so no watering; Pierre closed): forage Wild Horseradish, Daffodil, Leek and Dandelion, and fish the river. Some bundle fish bite only in rain.
   - Day 4: donate your first artifact or mineral to the Museum. Chop wood and fish.
   - Day 5: the Mines open (see `guide('mines')`). Harvest the 13 Parsnips: that is just enough XP for Farming 1 overnight, which unlocks the Scarecrow (50 Wood, 1 Coal, 20 Fiber; Clint sells Coal if the Mines give none). Replant the freed tiles with Potatoes, staying at 15 crops until the Scarecrow stands.
   - Day 6 on: place the Scarecrow in the middle of the field. Inside its range you can plant any number of crops. After your first Copper Ore, step onto the farm before 11:30 next morning: Clint visits then with the Furnace recipe.
3. **Open the Community Center between Days 5 and 11.**
   - On a dry day from Day 5, walk from the Bus Stop into Pelican Town between 8:00 and 13:00.
   - Then enter the Community Center and touch the golden scroll. Next morning the Wizard writes: visit his tower. Only then can you hand in bundles.
   - Use the first dry day so rain still leaves slack. Rooms: `guide('community_center')`.
4. **Egg Festival, Day 13.**
   - Morning: harvest the Cauliflower and water.
   - At the festival, shop before you talk to Lewis. He starts the egg hunt, which ends shopping. Strawberry Seeds (100g) are sold for gold only here. Event rules: `guide('festivals')`.
   - You get home at 22:00. That night hand in the bundle, then till, apply the 20 Speed-Gro, plant Strawberries on those tiles and water them.
   - Day-13 Strawberries on Speed-Gro give 3 harvests (Days 20, 24, 28). Without Speed-Gro they give 2 (Days 21, 25). Plant any extra Strawberries by Day 16 so they still give 2.
   - Size the plot to the tiles you can water every day, not to your cash. One Strawberry nets about 140g over 2 harvests or 260g over 3. Keep a small cash reserve.
5. **Seed choice.**
   - Cash is the limit but energy is not (before Day 13): Parsnips replanted every 4 days grow cash fastest per gold invested.
   - Watering or energy is the limit: Strawberries from Day 13, else Potato or Cauliflower (about equal per tile). Parsnips earn little per tile.
   - Green Bean only pays when planted early. Its trellis blocks walking, so put it on the field edge. Tulip and Blue Jazz are not money crops.
   - Hoe every Seed Spot you pass: through Day 23 they give Carrot Seeds (3 days, free), from Day 24 Summer Squash Seeds to keep for Summer 1.
   - Five Museum donations give 9 Cauliflower Seeds: plant them by Day 16. Ten give 9 Melon Seeds: keep them for Summer 1.
6. **Last planting day for a harvest by Day 28.** Every Spring crop dies on Summer 1.

   | Crop | Plant by |
   |---|---|
   | Carrot | Day 25 |
   | Parsnip | Day 24 |
   | Potato | Day 22 |
   | Strawberry | Day 20 (Day 16 for 2 harvests; one harvest barely repays the seed) |
   | Cauliflower | Day 16 |
   | Green Bean | Day 15 (2 harvests; a later bean loses money) |

7. **Upgrades.**
   - Large Pack (2,000g at Pierre's) is the first big purchase: every inventory shuffle costs actions. Buy it once cash covers it plus the Strawberry budget (usually Days 6-12, from fishing); otherwise after the first Strawberry harvest.
   - Clint keeps a tool for 2 days. Hand in the Watering Can only when tomorrow's forecast is rain. Upgrade order is in `guide('progression')`.
8. **Energy and money.**
   - Hand-water about 60-80 tiles at most. That uses roughly half your energy and leaves the rest for mining, fishing and clearing.
   - Check the TV forecast every morning. Spend rain days mining or fishing.
   - Be in bed before 24:00 to wake with full energy. Passing out (at 2:00 or from exhaustion) away from the farmhouse costs 10% of your gold, up to 1,000g.
   - Salmonberry bushes fruit on Days 15-18. Eat the berries as free energy instead of buying food.
9. **Days 21-28.** Mine for Copper and Coal, smelt bars, and clear the Summer field. Summer seeds go on sale only on Summer 1. Bank at least 80g (Blueberry or Melon Seeds) for each tile you can water. Summer 1 plans are in `guide('summer')`.

## Keep / Don't sell
- Spring Crops: 1 each of Parsnip, Green Bean, Cauliflower and Potato. Keep spares until it is handed in.
- Spring Foraging: 1 each of Wild Horseradish, Daffodil, Leek and Dandelion. It pays 30 Spring Seeds. If one is missing by Day 24, Pierre's Flower Dance stall sells Daffodil and Dandelion (see `guide('festivals')`).
- Gold-quality Parsnips, up to 5, for the Quality Crops bundle.
- The first catch of each fish, until `bundles` says it is not needed.
- Wood and Stone. The Construction bundle takes 2 x 99 Wood, 99 Stone and 10 Hardwood; Chests, Scarecrows and buildings eat more.
- The first copy of every artifact and mineral, for the Museum.
- If the save uses remixed bundles, the lists differ. Check `bundles(action=keep)` before selling.

## Avoid
- Buying Cauliflower after Day 1, or reaching Day 3 without a Bean Starter planted. Either breaks the Day-13 plan. JojaMart sells the Bean Starter on Day 3 at 25% more; otherwise buy seeds at Pierre's.
- A 16th crop before a Scarecrow covers the field: a crow can eat the only Cauliflower.
- Clearing the whole farm early or working until exhausted. Clear only what you plant this week.
- Planting more Strawberries or Potatoes than you can water daily. A dry day delays every harvest.
- Pierre's Speed-Gro (on sale from Day 15) for Strawberries. Another seed earns more than the extra harvest.
- Going into the Mines without food.

## Look it up
- `crops(season=spring)` gives seed prices from every shop, growth days, regrowth and value per tile. `crops(season=spring, fertilizer=speed_gro)` gives the Speed-Gro dates.
- `bundles(room=Pantry)` and `bundles(room=Crafts Room)` show what is still missing. `bundles(action=keep)` gives the ship-or-keep check for the whole pack.
- `item_info(name)` shows where an item is found (`Found:`), what to keep it for (`Keep for:`) and its sell price.
- `fish_info()` shows what bites here now, including rain-only bundle fish.
- `progress()` shows Farming XP and next unlocks (Scarecrow at Farming 1), tool upgrade costs with bars on hand, and Museum count toward the next reward.
- `calendar` shows festival days and birthdays.
