# Money: crops, processing and spending

## When this matters
- Every time you buy seeds, machines or saplings, choose a profession, or fill a Keg or Jar.
- Every time you sell: check what to keep first.

## Priorities
1. **Spend only on the current bottleneck.** Income = crop value x processing gain x tiles you can water and harvest each day. Find the limit and buy only for it:
   - Year 1 spring: tiles and seeds. Buy seeds while you have tilled, waterable tiles; leave the rest of the cash alone.
   - Watering energy and time: sprinklers (priority 4).
   - Once machines are running: more crops in, or more Jars/Kegs, whichever runs out first.
   - Year 2: greenhouse space and Keg count (see `guide('year2')`).
2. **Follow the Year-1 crop route.** Strawberries bought on Spring 13 (see `guide('spring')`), Blueberries on Summer 1 (see `guide('summer')`), Cranberries and Pumpkins on Fall 1 (see `guide('fall')`). Hold a cash reserve for each Day 1 seed buy. Fall is the biggest Year-1 payday.
3. **Planting rules.**
   - Plant a regrowing crop only if at least 2 regrowths still fit before Day 28. Late in a season, plant fast single-harvest crops that finish by Day 28.
   - Crops die when the season changes, except multi-season crops entering a season they allow.
   - Speed-Gro shortens only the first growth. Use it only when it adds a whole extra harvest before Day 28; never on a regrowing crop that is already producing.
   - Plant only what you can water every day: a missed day delays every harvest.
   - Put Basic Fertilizer (Farming 1, craft from 2 Sap) on high-value crops you will sell raw: better quality sells for more (`crops` values assume normal quality; `item_info` gives the silver/gold prices).
4. **Sprinklers are the Year-1 target.** Quality Sprinkler (Farming 6: 1 Iron Bar, 1 Gold Bar, 1 Refined Quartz) waters the 8 tiles around it. Reach Farming 6 by mid-summer and have 20+ before Year 2 spring. Make few basic Sprinklers. Mine for the bars (see `guide('mines')`, `guide('progression')`).
5. **Process everything you can.** Mass-produce Preserves Jars first (Farming 4: 50 Wood, 40 Stone, 8 Coal; cheap, no bars). Add Kegs at Farming 8 (30 Wood, 1 Copper Bar, 1 Iron Bar, 1 Oak Resin). Oak Resin limits Keg count: set Tappers on Oak trees early.
6. **Choose the machine by what is short:**
   - Jar: fruit or vegetable -> base x2 + 50 in about 3 days (2.8).
   - Keg: fruit -> Wine, base x3 in about 6-7 days; vegetable -> Juice, base x2.25 in about 4 days.
   - **Machines are the limit (more crops than machines, the usual case):** Jar beats Keg per machine-day for every fruit and vegetable. Fill Jars first; sell the overflow raw.
   - **Crops are the limit (machines sit empty):** put high-value fruit (Starfruit, Ancient Fruit, Melon, Strawberry, Rhubarb, Pomegranate) in Kegs; Wine is worth more per fruit. Fruit at base 50g or less (Blueberry, Salmonberry, Blackberry, Apricot) always goes to Jars: Jelly pays the same or more, much faster.
   - Vegetables: Jar, unless base is above 200g and Kegs are idle (Pumpkin Juice beats Pickles by only a little).
   - **Always Keg:** Hops (Pale Ale, about 1.5 days), Wheat (Beer, about 1 day), Coffee Beans (5 -> Coffee in 2 hours), cheap honey (Mead in 10 hours). These beat any Jar use.
   - Tea Leaves: Keg them (Green Tea in 3 hours) when Kegs are free; when leaves are short, a Jar pays more per leaf (Pickles 150g vs Green Tea 100g).
   - **Dehydrator for overflow fruit** (recipe from Pierre, 5,000g): once Jars are full, dry surplus fruit (5 fruit -> base x7.5 + 25 in 1 day, an artisan good, keeps quality) instead of selling it raw. Never take fruit away from an empty Jar for it.
   - **Jars and Kegs ignore quality.** When machines are the limit, process normal-quality crops and sell gold and iridium raw (silver x1.25, gold x1.5, iridium x2).
7. **Professions.** Farming 5: Tiller (+10% on crops and tree fruit; needed for Artisan). Farming 10: Artisan (+40% on all artisan goods: wine, juice, jelly, pickles, honey, mead, ale, cheese, mayo). Do not take Rancher if you plan Artisan. Pick Agriculturist instead only when you have very few machines. Other skills: see `guide('progression')`.
8. **Ship by default, sell at a shop when you need cash today.** The price is the same. A shop pays at once (seed days, the Egg Festival, before a big purchase); each shop buys only its own categories (Pierre: crops, forage, flowers, artisan goods, animal products, cooking; Willy: fish; Clint: ores, bars, gems, minerals; Marnie: animal products). The shipping bin pays overnight, takes any sellable item, and is the only way to fill the Shipping collection.
9. **Traveling Cart (Forest, Friday and Sunday): buy only two things.**
   - The Rare Seed in spring or summer (1,000g). Plant it Fall 1-4 for one Sweet Gem Berry (3,000g, 24 days).
   - A missing bundle item you cannot get in time any other way.
   - Random items cost at least 3x their base price, often far more. Compare with `item_info` before buying.
10. **Side incomes, in this order, never ahead of crops and Kegs:**
    1. **Bee House** (Farming 3: 40 Wood, 8 Coal, 1 Iron Bar, 1 Maple Syrup): 1 honey every 4 days, outdoors, not in winter. Honey = 100 + 2 x the base price of the closest mature flower within 5 tiles. Fairy Rose honey (680g) and Poppy honey (380g): sell raw. Any honey under 300g: Keg it into Mead (always 300g). Collect the honey before you harvest the flower.
    2. **Tappers** (Foraging 4: 40 Wood, 2 Copper Bars) are a materials source, not profit: Oak Resin every 7 days (one per Keg), Maple Syrup every 9 days (one per Bee House), Pine Tar every 5 days (Speed-Gro).
    3. **Crab Pots** (Fishing 3: 40 Wood, 3 Iron Bars): craft them; never buy them at 1,500g. They need bait every day. They are for the Crab Pot bundle and Fishing XP, weak money (see `guide('fishing')`).
    4. **Fruit trees** are slow: a month to mature, then 1 fruit a day in their season only. A 6,000g Peach returns about 3,900g in its first summer, more as its fruit quality rises each year. Buy saplings after the Keg engine runs (bundle saplings: see `guide('community_center')`); plant them on the greenhouse border in Year 2 (see `guide('year2')`).
    - Animals: see `guide('animals')`.

## Keep / Don't sell
- Before any bulk sale, keep 1 of each item an open bundle still needs, at the quality it needs; a missed seasonal item costs a year. Run `bundles(action=keep)`.
- Keep gold-quality crops for the Quality Crops bundle and the Fair grange (see `guide('festivals')`).
- Never sell Wood, Stone, Hardwood, Coal or Oak Resin: Jars, Kegs, buildings and sprinklers need them in bulk, and Oak Resin is the Keg bottleneck. Wood and Stone sell for 2g.
- Keep the first Sweet Gem Berry for the Old Master Cannoli statue in the Secret Woods (Stardrop, +34 max energy); sell later ones.
- Keep Maple Syrup for Bee Houses and Copper, Iron and Gold Bars for sprinklers and Kegs.
- Keep normal-quality fruit and vegetables for your Jars and Kegs; sell the gold and iridium ones raw when the machines are full.

## Avoid
- Buying a coop or animals early in Year 1, furniture, decor, the 10,000g backpack, or Joja items.
- Buying random Traveling Cart items.
- Planting more than you can water, or crops that cannot mature by Day 28.
- Upgrading the Watering Can mid-season with a big field (you lose it for 2 days); do it before forecast rain or on the last day of a season.
- Kegging Blueberries, Cranberries, Salmonberries or Blackberries while Jars could take them.
- Kegging Fairy Rose or Poppy honey (Mead is worth less).
- Putting gold or iridium crops in machines while normal ones wait.
- Buying Crab Pots, or leaving them unbaited.
- Taking Rancher at Farming 5 when you plan Artisan.
- Speed-Gro on a regrowing crop that already produces.

## Look it up
- `crops(season, fertilizer)`: profit per tile and per day for every crop, seed prices in every shop and last planting day (raw value only; `machines(item=X)` for Jar/Keg value).
- `machines(item=X)`: every machine that takes X, with output price, days and value per machine-day. `machines()`: which placed machines are ready, working or empty.
- `item_info(name)`: sell price with your professions, where to get it, what to keep it for, best machine outputs.
- `bundles(action=keep)`: ship-or-keep check for every item in your pack (chest stacks count toward bundle slots first).
- `progress()`: Farming level, XP to the next level, the next craft unlocks and professions.
